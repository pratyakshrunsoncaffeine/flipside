Official rubric:
- Use of Switch, 30%: is Switch central, and would the project stop working without it?
- Technical execution, 25%: does it run, and could someone clone the repo and reproduce it?
- Creativity and impact, 25%: is it a fresh idea that solves a problem a real team has?

The panel includes Switch engineers at SandboxAQ (a security company), DevTools Academy, and WeMakeDevs.
They reward Switch being load-bearing, honest claims, reproducibility, and safe agent behaviour; they distrust staged demos and ideas that a plain bot or CI job could do.
